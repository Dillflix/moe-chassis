MOE — STEP 22 / LOW DUCT RETAINING BAR
Revision 26 — 23 October 2026

Print one bar. This replaces the tall Step 20 FAN_BANK_CARRIER at the fan
intake end. No chassis reprint, new heatserts, or changes to the duct prints.

POSITION / DIMENSIONS (mm)
The front/intake faces of the duct fans contact the rear face of the bar.
That contact face is EXACTLY 10.00 mm behind the centers of the two front
floor heatserts. Feet project FORWARD, toward the six front chassis fans.

Part envelope: 240 wide x 19 fore-aft x 24 tall above the chassis floor.
Vertical bar thickness: 6.00. Floor feet: 4.00 thick.
Two open-top scallops follow 120.00 mm diameter circles.
The 2.00 mm sole connects the bar along the fan-frame bottom edges.
It sits below the nominal 115 mm airway; the two small forward feet and
floor screw heads occupy a little space near the lower inlet rim.
The upper two fans have no retaining bar in front of them.

CAD axes: +X is chassis left, +Y front, +Z up.
Floor Z = 19.05. Floor anchor centers: (260, 520) and (400, 520); 140 pitch.
Fan front / bar contact plane Y = 510. Bar body runs Y 510–516.
Feet reach Y 529. Fan center X = 252 and 372; lower center Z = 79.05.
Four fan holes: X 199.5, 304.5, 319.5, 424.5 at Z 26.55.
Fan pattern is unchanged, 105 mm horizontally. Bar holes are 45-degree
diamonds with a 3.4 mm inscribed diameter for support-free small roofs.
Floor holes are 3.4 mm round. A 10 mm driver envelope clears vertically.

The existing reference geometry puts the rear of the front chassis fans at
Y 554.2: 44.2 mm (1.74 inches) ahead of the duct-fan intake faces. Your 1.5-inch
measurement was approximate; the clarified 10 mm heatsert offset controls.

PRINT
Use MOE_Duct_Retaining_Bar_Step22_Print.stl at 100% scale.
The supplied STL is already underside down. Feet flat on bed; bar upright.
PETG, 0.20 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers, 40% infill are a
reasonable starting profile. A brim can help this long, narrow print.
No generated support is intended. The 45-degree mesh screen passes.
Print only this STL; the context STEP contains reference parts and hardware.

HARDWARE
Floor: 2 M3×8 socket cap screws + 2 washers, 0.5 mm thick, OD <=7 mm.
This reuses the former carrier floor-screw stack. Install heatserts flush.
Nominal thread penetration 3.5 mm; blind-pocket bottom clearance 1.7 mm.

Bar to fans/ducts: 4 M3×45 through-bolts, 8 washers, and 4 M3 nuts.
This length assumes ONE 25 mm fan stage plus the 6 mm bar and the existing
6 mm duct screw seats. Washers are 0.5 mm thick, OD <=7; M3 nuts are 5.5 AF
and 2.4 mm thick. Fit washers at both head and nut.

Removing the former 20 mm carrier changes all fan-bolt stack lengths.
For the remaining 12 fan positions, M3×40 is the nominal single-stage
selection with the same washers and nuts. Do not blindly reuse the old
M3×55 bolts: their extra reach may contact the duct recess bottoms.
If your actual fan thickness or stack differs, choose length to suit.

INSTALL — KEEP THE FIDDLY WORK ON THE BENCH
1. Remove the tall fan carrier. Keep any rear supports that are carrying
   the duct bodies; this part locates and retains their front/fan ends.
2. With both fan/duct modules outside the chassis, place their intake faces
   against the back of the bar. Its two floor feet point toward you/front.
3. Insert the four M3×45 bolts from the bar/front side through the two lower
   corner holes of EACH LOWER fan and into the existing duct corner seats.
   Fit washers and nuts in the accessible duct recesses. Leave slightly loose.
   The upper fans use their existing duct mounts, with appropriate bolt length.
4. Lower the joined assembly into the chassis. Let the GPU ends seat
   naturally. Line the bar feet up with the two front floor inserts.
5. From directly above, install the two M3×8 floor screws with washers.
   Snug the floor screws and four bar bolts after checking alignment.
6. Check that fan rotors turn freely and the GPU ends are not being pulled
   into position by the fasteners. This is a locating/retaining bar, not a
   replacement for any rear support needed to carry the duct weight.
